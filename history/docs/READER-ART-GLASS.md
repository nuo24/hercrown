# Reader illustration and Discover glass pass

The Bible reader uses chapter-opening cutouts and a small selection of passage-specific art. Genesis opens with a seedling, Psalms with an olive sprig, Proverbs with a pearl, and John with praying hands; other chapters use the open book. Psalm 23:1, Psalm 119:105, Proverbs 31:25 and Matthew 11:28 use their relevant existing illustrations. Images sit above the verse in normal layout flow, reserve their dimensions and remain decorative to accessibility. They never cover Scripture or act as controls.

Discover’s title card now has a translucent tinted background, 18px backdrop blur, restrained edge highlights and layered soft shadows. Dark mode and reduced-transparency fallbacks are included. No continuous animation was added.

Browser verification: Genesis opening art, scrolling to verse 3 with updated rail, Discover title card in light/dark mode. Reader navigation tests passed. Browser demo changes only.
