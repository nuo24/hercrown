# Passage study and Home refinements

The two Home cards serve different jobs. Today’s Reading is a compact, image-led route into a multi-passage plan. The larger serif reflection card is an invitation to pause with one passage. Keeping both supports guided and spontaneous reading but costs vertical space and risks repetition. The second card is now labeled “A moment of reflection” and its copy explicitly points to Psalm 23.

Home now says “Hi, Nate!” and its appearance control is replaced by a flame/count button opening streak details. Appearance remains in Settings; the external preview toolbar also retains its testing toggle.

Each reader verse now has an outlined book/search study button above its heart button. It opens a bottom sheet with Explain easier, Deeper study and Related verses. The current verse and KJV attribution remain visible. Deeper study uses the included surrounding chapter text and reflection prompt. Plain-language notes are authored reading aids for the nine featured passages and first three Genesis verses; remaining passages honestly fall back to context. Related verses are explicitly selected companion passages, not a claimed scholarly cross-reference database. Original-language tools and version comparison are marked unavailable in this KJV-only sample.

Verified in browser: Home greeting, flame details, Settings appearance control, reader study menu and deeper-study context. Existing onboarding, scroll and streak tests pass. Browser mockup implementation only.
