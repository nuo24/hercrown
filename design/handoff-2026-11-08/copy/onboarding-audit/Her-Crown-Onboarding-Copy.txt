HER CROWN — CURRENT ONBOARDING COPY + CONDITIONAL LOGIC
Extracted 8 November 2026, from the context26 preview build.

PURPOSE
This is an extraction of what is implemented, not a proposed rewrite. Use it to analyze voice, clarity, personalization, psychological effects, repetition, Christian framing, factual claims, and all conditional copy. Do not assume a sentence described here is approved merely because it exists in the prototype.

READING KEY
{name} = first name after trimming and uppercasing the first letter.
{burden} = the user's own words, trimmed.
{people} = the free-text prayer recipient entry, trimmed.
Line breaks are preserved below where deliberately authored. Text wraps further according to screen width.
Italics and emphasis are described explicitly. Braces denote variables, not literal text.
Internal step numbers are not sequential, but the current user journey has 14 screens.

SHARED COPY AND BEHAVIOR
Screens 3–14 show the centered header “A moment for you”. Several other kicker strings exist in source but are ignored by the rendering function; they are NOT displayed. See source-only copy at the end.
Back control accessible label: “Back”.
Questions 4–11 have a top-right “Skip”.
Continue buttons on most screens include a decorative arrow. Free-text pages use an arrow-only button with the accessible label “Continue”.
Progress accessible label: “Onboarding”; 14 logical steps are represented by eight visual segments.
Acknowledgments appear on the next screen, before its question. They are not separate navigation steps. They finish typing and exit before the question begins. Options and input controls appear only after the question finishes typing. Reduced Motion presents the complete content immediately.
Skip advances without an acknowledgment. Back clears the acknowledgment. Existing answers survive Back; Skip does NOT clear an answer previously entered on a revisited page.
There is no required-answer validation, missing-answer error, or minimum character count. All questions can be left blank.

PRAYER / EXACT TEMPLATE
Dear God,

Thank You for {name-or-fallback}. {season-sentence} {need-sentence}

{loved-one-sentence} {optional-own-words-sentence}Let her feel loved and draw closer to You.

Amen.

Name fallback, exact casing: “Your daughter”.
This results in “Thank You for Your daughter.” if no first name was provided.

Season sentence: FIRST applicable rule.
1. Motherhood selected, regardless of other seasons:
“Hold her as she cares for her children.”
2. Separated or divorced selected without Motherhood:
“Be near as she finds her footing after separation.”
3. Otherwise, first selected option with a mapped sentence, in selection order:
Single → “Walk with her as she builds her life.”
Dating → “Guide her heart in love.”
Married → “Nurture tenderness in her marriage.”
4. No mapped selection / Prefer not to say:
“Meet her right where she is.”

Need sentence:
No heart selections → “Give her rest for today and courage for tomorrow.”
One or more → “Give her {mapped phrase(s) joined with comma+space}.”
Peace → “peace for her heart”
Purpose → “direction for her next step”
Faith → “trust amid her questions”
Relationships → “kindness in her relationships”
Identity → “confidence in her worth”
Anxiety → “calm in anxious moments”
Gratitude → “eyes for everyday goodness”
Hope → “hope for tomorrow”
Wisdom → “wisdom for her choices”
Prayer joins every selected phrase with commas only; it does not add “and” before the last phrase. Selection order is retained.

Loved-one sentence:
If prayer recipient text entered → “Please watch over {people}.”
Otherwise if legacy children field has text → “Please watch over {children}.”
Otherwise if legacy people array contains a value other than “Me” → “Hold her loved ones close, too.”
Otherwise → empty.
The current 14-screen UI does not populate children or people, so those legacy branches are not reachable through its questions.

Own-words sentence:
If burden nonblank → “Hear what weighs on her: “{burden}” ”
There is a trailing space after the closing curly quote, and no automatic full stop after the quote. The next sentence is appended immediately after that space.
If blank → empty.
The raw composed string includes a space after the loved-one slot even when that slot is empty. Preserve this when auditing exact output, though HTML collapses it visually.

What does NOT change the prayer:
Comparison answer, Bible familiarity, daily time, reminder choice. Those affect acknowledgments or other app preferences, not the prayer's wording.
The prayer is locally assembled from these templates; it is not a remote AI-generated prayer.

Reminder permission edges:
Off → save disabled preference, no permission prompt, advance.
On + browser Notification API + permission default → request OS/browser permission on Save, await response, then advance.
On + permission granted → no new permission prompt, advance.
On + permission denied → no new permission prompt, still advance; no denial/error copy.
Notification API unavailable → advance; no unsupported-state copy.
Preference is stored before permission resolves. Denial does not automatically change enabled=true back to false. This is a prototype behavior to evaluate.
No native reminder scheduling occurs in this browser prototype. Actual iOS permission wording is system-owned and not authored here.

EDGE-CASE CHECKLIST FOR THE ANALYZER
• Empty name / whitespace-only name → no personalized prefix; prayer uses “Your daughter”.
• Lowercase initial name → capitalized on Next; mixed/all-uppercase remainder remains unchanged.
• Name beginning with non-letter punctuation → initial capitalization rule does not capitalize a later letter.
• Long names / burden / recipient text → character limits above, with no truncation warning copy.
• User-entered markup → HTML escaped in visible content; raw text retained for the prayer download.
• Skip after revisiting answered question → acknowledgment suppressed, prior answer still retained and can affect prayer.
• Back → preserves answers, clears previous acknowledgment; name page introductory message always replays.
• Season selections → multiple potentially conflicting statuses allowed; acknowledgment and prayer use different precedence.
• Prefer not to say → exclusive with other season selections; no acknowledgment and generic prayer season.
• Four heart chips attempted → limit copy; three remain selected.
• Zero heart selections → generic prayer need sentence.
• Burden skipped → no quoted burden in prayer, unless an earlier answer remained after Back/Skip.
• Prayer recipient skipped → no recipient sentence, unless an earlier answer remained after Back/Skip.
• No speech support → visible speech control has no unsupported-state explanation.
• Reminder denied / missing API / storage failure → no authored error feedback.
• Closing or restarting → answers are held in memory; prayer/reminder preferences may have been stored separately. Restart does not explicitly clear the in-memory answers object.
• Reduced Motion → no typing delay or haptic ticks; complete copy/control presentation.
• App/browser backgrounding → animations settle to visible content, avoiding a stuck hidden Continue.
• Guest in full app → immediate reader, no personalized prayer/completion screen.
• Guest in standalone study → attempts to store default prayer, then shows completion instead.
• No account, sign-in, email, age, denomination, rating, sharing requirement, or mandatory payment question occurs in this current flow.
