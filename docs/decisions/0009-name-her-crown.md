# 0009: The name is Her Crown

**Date:** 2026-11-03 · **Status:** accepted (owner and cofounder)

**Decision:** The app is **Her Crown**. The App Store name chosen is "Her Crown: Scroll the Bible". The company contact is macnatephil@gmail.com.

**Where the name appears:**
- **User-facing text says "Her Crown"**: emails, notifications, legal pages, errors, the subscription ("Her Crown Plus"), the notification link scheme `hercrown://` and support references (`HC-…`).
- **Internal identifiers keep "biblescroll"** where users never see them: the repo folders, Worker and database names. The bundle id is `com.hercrown.app` (registered 2026-11-03) and the product ids are `hercrown.plus.yearly`, `hercrown.plus.weekly`, `hercrown.plus.yearly.offer` (App Store Connect app id 6830437910).


**Risk noted to the owner:** "Scroll the Bible" is the competitor's brand. A name that contains it can be rejected (App Store 4.1 / 5.2) or draw a trademark complaint after launch. The lower-risk version is the name "Her Crown" with "Scroll the Bible for Women" in the subtitle and keywords. The owner decides before submitting.

**Logo direction:** the crown of thorns with flowers, from the owner's sketches. It must work at 60 px.
