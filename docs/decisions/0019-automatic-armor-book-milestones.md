# 0019 — Automatic book milestones for Armor of God

Date: 2026-11-22. Owner approved replacing Armor's prescribed chapter journeys with the Fruit collection's automatic medal format, while retaining its artistic presentation.

Armor keeps all six supplied illustrations, carousel, icon tiles, colors, haptics and rarity treatment. Each piece displays its Ephesians reference: belt/breastplate 6:14, shoes 6:15, shield 6:16, helmet/sword 6:17. References are labels; Scripture text is not modified.

Goals are 1, 3, 5, 10, 20 and 40 distinct completed canonical Bible books, paired with existing minimums of 21, 30, 45, 60, 90 and 180 reading days. Any books count; no required-reading list or assigned route. Only server-recorded fully completed books with matching canonical chapter counts qualify. Partial, duplicate, unknown and malformed book records cannot grant extra credit.

Fruits, Psalms, Women and Promises goals remain unchanged. Award cache rules version 3 recalculates Armor awards from current verified progress, preserving unchanged collection dates. Older pre-journey cache migration remains supported. This supersedes decision 0017 for Armor only.

Include this and the tested onboarding tap-to-finish change in TestFlight 1.0.1 (36), staging only. Public release and actual gift checkout still require their separate validation.
