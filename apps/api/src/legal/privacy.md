# Privacy Policy

_Draft, last updated November 3, 2026. Have it reviewed before launch._

Her Crown ("we", "us") is made by Nathan Okolo, an individual developer. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Her Crown app and why. Questions: macnatephil@gmail.com.

## The short version

- You can read without an account. We create an anonymous guest account so your saves and streak are kept.
- We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
- Your notes, reading history and onboarding answers are used only to run the app for you.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account.

## What we collect

**Account.** A random account ID. If you sign in with Apple, your Apple account identifier and the email Apple shares, which may be a private relay address.

**Profile and preferences (optional).** Display name, bio, profile photo, your onboarding answers (intention, familiarity, topics, reading goal), reminder time and time zone, appearance, font size and translation.

**Reading activity.** Verses you save, your notes on verses, days you read and opened the app, chapters you finish, reading-plan progress, devotionals you read, and study tools you open.

**Purchases.** If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We receive the subscription's product, status, dates and Apple transaction identifiers, from Apple and from our subscription provider, so we know whether you have Plus. We never see your payment details.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, your device's push token and time zone, so we can remind you before a free trial ends.

**Support messages.** What you write to us, the category, and the email you give for a reply.

**Usage and diagnostics.** Events such as "paywall viewed", "trial started" or "a study was opened", with a pseudonymous ID, an install ID, app version, platform and country, plus error reports. These never include your name, email, notes, onboarding answers or which verses you read.

## How we use it

To provide the app: sync your reading across devices, keep your streak, unlock Plus, send reminders you asked for, and answer support requests. To understand and improve the app in aggregate, for example how many people finish onboarding or start a trial. To keep the service secure, for example rate limits and abuse prevention.

## AI study tools

When you open Explain Easier or Deeper Study for a verse, we send the verse and passages from public-domain commentaries and Bible-study datasets to an AI model provider. **We do not send your notes, your profile or anything that identifies you.** Answers are stored and shared with everyone who studies the same verse.

## Who processes data for us

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, database and file storage.
- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple, payments, push notifications.
- **Superwall:** subscription paywall and purchase events.
- **Tinybird:** pseudonymous analytics and error reports.
- **OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to (DeepSeek):** AI study tools, receiving only verse and source text.
- **API.Bible:** if you read an online translation, it receives the passage request.

They may only use the data to provide their service to us.

## Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photo, preferences, saves, notes, reading history, plan progress, devices and support requests. Analytics events keep only the pseudonymous ID and are deleted automatically after 13 months. Apple keeps its own purchase records.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to some uses. Contact macnatephil@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

## Children

Her Crown isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We'll update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
