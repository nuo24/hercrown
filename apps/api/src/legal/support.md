# Her Crown Support

We're glad you're here. Write to us at **macnatephil@gmail.com**, or in the app at **Settings → Contact & Support**. We usually reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**I subscribed but don't have Plus.** Open **Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases**. Plus follows the Apple ID you bought it with. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the reference shown in Settings → Contact & Support.

**How do I cancel?** In iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Her Crown**. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends. You keep Plus until the end of the time you paid for.

**How do I get a refund?** Apple handles App Store payments, so refunds are requested at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). See our [Refund Policy](https://bible-scroll-api-staging.bible-scroll-api.workers.dev/legal/refunds).

**I got a new phone.** Sign in with Apple on the new phone (Settings → Account) to bring your saves, notes and streak with you, then restore your purchase.

**Can I read offline?** Yes. The King James Version, Berean Standard Bible and World English Bible are built into the app. Other translations need a connection.

**Are the study answers Scripture?** No. Explain Easier and Deeper Study are AI summaries of the public-domain commentaries cited with each answer. They're labeled in the app and can contain mistakes. Tap **Report a problem** under any answer and we'll review it.

**How do I delete my account?** **Settings → Account → Delete account** permanently removes your profile and reading data. If you subscribe, cancel in iPhone Settings too, because deleting your account doesn't stop Apple billing.
